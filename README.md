# Kisan Setu 🌾

### Digital Procurement & Mandi Management Platform for Farmers

Kisan Setu is a digital platform designed to streamline the agricultural procurement process by connecting farmers with mandi/procurement centers through a transparent, trackable, and technology-driven workflow.

The platform digitizes the complete procurement journey, from farmer registration and slot booking to token generation, geofenced check-in, weighment, quality assessment, receipt generation, and payment/DBT tracking.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional agricultural procurement workflows can involve:

- Long queues at procurement centers
- Unpredictable waiting times
- Manual registration and record keeping
- Lack of real-time queue visibility
- Manual weighment and quality recording
- Delays in payment tracking
- Limited transparency for farmers and administrators
- Difficulty maintaining a complete transaction history

Kisan Setu aims to address these issues through a unified digital procurement workflow.

---

# 🚀 Key Features

## 👨‍🌾 Farmer Portal

### 1. Farmer Registration & Verification
- Farmer registration through KCC/Aadhaar-related information
- Farmer profile creation
- Farmer identification through a stable Farmer ID
- Backend-based registration workflow

### 2. Slot Booking
Farmers can:
- Select procurement slots
- Enter crop and estimated quantity
- Provide vehicle information
- Receive a unique booking ID
- Receive a digital procurement token

### 3. Digital Token
Each booking generates a unique token that can be used throughout the procurement lifecycle.
